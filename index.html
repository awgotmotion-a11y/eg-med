About Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)

Medication-Assisted Treatment combines FDA-approved medications like Suboxone (buprenorphine/naloxone) with compassionate clinical care to treat opioid use disorder. MAT is the most evidence-based approach available, reducing withdrawal symptoms, cravings, and the risk of overdose. Dr. Wolfington provides non-judgmental evaluations and ongoing MAT management. Walk-ins are accepted for initial consultations.

What to expect: Dr. Wolfington will review your history, discuss your goals, and determine an appropriate starting plan. Follow-up appointments monitor progress and adjust care as needed. There is no shame here. This is healthcare.

About Medical Marijuana Certification in Florida

Florida's Medical Marijuana Use Registry allows qualified patients to legally purchase and use cannabis products from any licensed dispensary in the state. Dr. Wolfington is a licensed Florida certifying physician. Your certification is entered into the state registry same day or next business day, and you can then apply for your official MMUR ID card online through the Florida Department of Health.

Florida's qualifying conditions (Fla. Stat. 381.986):

  • Cancer
  • Epilepsy
  • Glaucoma
  • HIV / AIDS
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • ALS (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis)
  • Crohn's disease
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Chronic nonmalignant pain caused by, or originating from, a qualifying condition
  • A terminal condition diagnosed by another physician
  • Conditions of the same kind or class as those listed

Whether you qualify is decided by the physician at your evaluation. Certification is not guaranteed.

Your right to a Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining how much your medical care will cost. Under the law, health care providers need to give patients who don't have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services. If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill.

Read the full notice or visit cms.gov/nosurprises.

What we take down when you book

Whether you book by phone or at the front desk, we write down the same few things, and nothing more:

  • Your name and date of birth
  • A phone number we can reach you on
  • Whether this is your first visit with us
  • What you would like to be seen for, in general terms
  • The days and times that suit you

Bring a photo ID and a list of your current medications to the visit itself. We are a self-pay clinic, so there is no insurance card to bring. If you would like the cost in writing beforehand, ask for a Good Faith Estimate.

Appointment details are kept in the clinic's own records and treated as part of your medical record, under our Notice of Privacy Practices. Please save sensitive medical detail for your visit rather than the booking call. If you cannot make it, call and let us know so we can give the time to someone else. For a medical emergency, call 911.
