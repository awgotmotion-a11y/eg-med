1. The short answer

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and uses no analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or similar tracking technologies. That is why it does not show a cookie banner.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save in your browser. Similar technologies include browser storage, pixels and tracking scripts. Websites often use them to remember visitors or to follow them across the internet.

3. Fonts and other files

The typefaces, images, scripts and 3D graphics on this site are served from eaumed.com itself. Browsing the site does not cause your browser to contact Google, Facebook or any other third party.

4. Links you click

If you follow a link to another website, such as Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram or the supplement dispensary, that website may set its own cookies under its own policy. Nothing from those services loads on our pages.

5. Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies for any website in your browser settings. Doing so will not affect this site, because it does not use them.

6. Changes to this notice
